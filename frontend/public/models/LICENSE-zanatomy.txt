Changes made by Smartious (2026): converted from Blender to glTF binary with
tools/models/blend2glb.py; kept a selection of structures from the cardiovascular,
visceral and nervous systems (label lines, text and helper objects removed);
the brain simplified to about 1 mm; the muscles (with the skeleton) simplified to about
3.5 mm, keeping only the muscles and bones that can be seen from outside (tools/models/muscles-v1.names.txt); the right knee cut out of the atlas between
0.33 m and 0.53 m above the ground, and the right hip above 0.66 m; converted to a Y-up coordinate system; smooth
normals computed; positions stored as 16-bit integers (KHR_mesh_quantization);
materials replaced with plain colours.
The descriptions shown in the lab are Smartious's own and are not part of these files.
