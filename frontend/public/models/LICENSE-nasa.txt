saturn-v-v1.glb, apollo-lunar-module-v1.glb, perseverance-v1.glb, ingenuity-v1.glb, iss-v1.glb, hubble-v1.glb
— spacecraft models used in the Smartious Virtual Lab (/lab/models)

Source: NASA 3D Resources, https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources
  ("Saturn V", "Apollo Lunar Module", "Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover", "Ingenuity Mars Helicopter", "International Space Station (ISS) (A)", "Hubble Space Telescope (A)")
NASA describes these assets as "free and without copyright". Credit: NASA.

Use follows NASA's media usage guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/):
Smartious is not endorsed by NASA, and the models are used for education only.
The NASA insignia ("meatball") and logotype ("worm") and the JPL logo, whose use is
restricted, were painted out of the textures of perseverance-v1.glb and ingenuity-v1.glb
(tools/models/glb_patch_images.py). saturn-v-v1.glb, apollo-lunar-module-v1.glb, iss-v1.glb and
hubble-v1.glb are NASA's files unchanged. The labels and descriptions shown in the lab are Smartious's own.
